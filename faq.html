🛍️ Orders

How do I place an order? +
Simple. Browse the shop, pick your items and sizes, add them to your bag, fill in your delivery details on the cart page, choose your payment method, and hit Place Order. You'll then send your payment screenshot via WhatsApp to confirm.
Can I order via WhatsApp? +
Yes! You can click the Order via WhatsApp button in your cart and it'll send us your full order details automatically. We'll confirm availability and send you payment info.
How do I know my order was confirmed? +
After you place your order and send your payment screenshot via WhatsApp, we'll confirm your order within 1-2 hours (during business hours 10am–8pm). You'll get a WhatsApp reply with your confirmation.
Can I cancel or modify my order? +
You can cancel or modify your order before it's dispatched. Message us on WhatsApp ASAP with your order number. Once it's shipped, we can't make changes.

📦 Shipping

How long does delivery take? +
Orders are dispatched within 24 hours of payment confirmation. Delivery takes 3-5 business days nationwide depending on your city. Major cities like Karachi, Lahore, and Islamabad are usually faster.
What's the shipping cost? +
Shipping is PKR 200 on all orders. Orders above PKR 2,000 get FREE shipping — so add one more item and you're sorted.
Do you deliver nationwide? +
Yes — we deliver all across Pakistan. Even remote areas, just expect delivery to take a day or two longer.
How do I track my order? +
Once your order is dispatched, we'll send you a tracking number via WhatsApp. You can use it on the courier's website to track your package.

💸 Payments

What payment methods do you accept? +
We accept JazzCash, EasyPaisa, and Cash on Delivery (COD) in select cities. More payment options coming soon.
How does JazzCash/EasyPaisa payment work? +
1. Place your order on the website
2. Send the exact total to our JazzCash or EasyPaisa number (shown during checkout)
3. Take a screenshot of the transaction
4. Send the screenshot to our WhatsApp
5. We confirm and dispatch within 24 hours ✅
Is Cash on Delivery available? +
Yes, COD is available in major cities. Select it during checkout. Note: COD orders may take slightly longer to process. COD fee of PKR 50 may apply.
What if I sent the wrong amount? +
Message us on WhatsApp immediately with your order number and the transaction screenshot. We'll sort it out for you.

↩️ Returns

Can I return or exchange my order? +
Yes. You have 7 days from receiving your order to request a return or exchange. The item must be unused, unwashed, and in its original condition with tags attached.
How do I initiate a return? +
Message us on WhatsApp with:
• Your order number
• Photos of the item
• Reason for return

We'll guide you through the rest. Return shipping cost is on the customer unless the item is defective or wrong.
What if I received a wrong or defective item? +
That's on us. Message us immediately with photos and we'll send a replacement or full refund — no questions asked.

👕 Products

How do I find my size? +
Our pieces are oversized/relaxed fit. If you're between sizes, go down a size. When in doubt, message us on WhatsApp with your measurements and we'll help you pick.
Are your products restocked? +
Some items are limited drops and won't restock. Follow us on Instagram and TikTok to get notified when new drops go live. Core items may restock — ask us on WhatsApp.
How do I care for my Nexo pieces? +
• Wash cold (30°C max)
• Turn inside out before washing
• Do not tumble dry — hang dry only
• Do not bleach
• Iron inside out on low heat
